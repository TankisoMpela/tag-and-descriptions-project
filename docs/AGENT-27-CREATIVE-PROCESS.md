# Agent 27 — thumbnail creative process

This guide describes the current image workflow. The old thumbnail
reimagination process and its flat-only, no-photography, centered-title rules
are retired. The authoritative operational instructions are in
`agents/agent-27-font-image-generator.md`.

## What Agent 27 produces

Agent 27 renders the exact font name in the actual font on a clean white field.
These images preserve the font's real letterforms as raw material for an image
model; they are not finished thumbnail designs. It also writes three complete,
font-specific prompts in `visual-direction/theme.txt`:

- 3:2 main marketplace thumbnail;
- independently composed 1:1 main thumbnail;
- separate 1:1 storefront thumbnail.

Each prompt is paired with the matching white-background specimen. No old
marketplace thumbnail is required or used.

## How to build a prompt

Read `font-factory/Style-References/thumbnail-prompt-research-2026-10-20/prompt-findings.md` and `reference-structure-audit.md`; the audit summarizes all 33 saved references and their full captions. Start from the actual white-background font specimen and one visible glyph trait. Choose one reference-backed composition mechanism that fits this font. All lettering stays a flat, front-facing overlay; never put the words onto a photographed or rendered surface.

Write concise, complete art direction. The old 80–140-word quota is retired; use only the words needed to describe the name and ratio, font-specific premise, title position/scale, its relationship to the scene, and essential local contrast/focus. The lettering is always a flat 2D overlay, including when the background is a real 3D scene; it never follows that scene's plane, perspective, lighting, or material. In a photo with a person, crop out the face or soften and subdue it behind the crisp title.

Preserve the name's spelling, natural capitalization, glyph shapes, spacing and filled/outlined construction. Keep important glyphs readable and inside the frame. Do not force supporting props or fill open areas. No glitter, sparkles, starbursts, confetti, particles, fake lens flare, arbitrary badges, gratuitous glow, excessive microtexture, or generic AI decoration. Avoid dark-on-dark and similar-value title/background colors. Read the research captions for what they actually show; do not copy a whole caption into a prompt. Compare the five most recently updated non-archived thumbnail plans across medium, layout, focal motif, palette, texture/light and title placement; inspect an actual image only when one exists. Privately develop three distinct directions, choose a font-specific one that differs across several dimensions, and record only a concise novelty note in `candidate-review.md`. Do not rely on generic glossy luxury still lifes as a default. For this seller, also avoid the recently rejected oversized cropped initial with orbit rings, blue/berry split descriptor panel, and arch/portal title frame as default recipes. A flat design can use quiet paper, ink, or print texture or a faint relevant illustration; keep it specific and secondary so the image feels authored without becoming busy or sterile.

## Review before handoff

Check that every prompt:

1. is complete enough to use with only its matching specimen;
2. describes one clear, font-specific premise with a restrained amount of detail;
3. gives the 3:2 and 1:1 main versions different compositions;
4. preserves the exact font name and does not add unrequested copy;
5. keeps the lettering legible, unclipped, and distinct from the background;
6. differs in subject, composition, and type gesture from recent prompts.

The model-generated final artwork is a later manual step. Inspect each result
for exact spelling and capitalization, preserved letterforms, crop, contrast,
small-size readability, visual coherence, and fidelity to its prompt before
approving it for the preparation folder.
