# Pixiv Tag and Discovery-Life Ledger

This is a persistent coverage/activity ledger, not a Pixiv search-volume,
sales, or conversion report. Keep rejected and low-activity terms so later
font products do not repeat the same dead research.

| Retrieval date | Product | Tag | Language | Exact tag/search URL | Fetched work URL | Relevant works visible | Distinct creators | Newest relevant date | Tag/use status | Tier | Provider/status/fallback | Product-fit decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---:|---:|---|---|---|---|---|

## Evidence rules

- Tier A: at least 5 relevant visible works from at least 2 creators, with at
  least one recent relevant work when dates are exposed.
- Tier B: 2–4 relevant works or a smaller sample with clear recency and two
  distinct creators.
- Tier C: one relevant work, old-only evidence, caption-only use, or ambiguous
  results; normally wording support only.
- REJECT: no relevant work, unrelated results, misleading product fit, or a
  blocked/error page without content.
- If no total is exposed, record `visible lower bound: N; total unknown`.
- Views, likes, bookmarks, ranking position, and visible result counts are
  coverage/comparison signals only, never Pixiv demand or sales volume.
