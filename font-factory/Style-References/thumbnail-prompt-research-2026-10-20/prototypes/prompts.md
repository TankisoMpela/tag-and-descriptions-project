# Thumbnail Prompts — 17-Font Sweep

Each render uses the matching font's white-background specimen as a lettering reference. The visible title and descriptor are listed exactly below. New words use title case; all-capital titles are kept only where the typeface's own specimen is designed that way. The descriptor is subordinate but large enough to read at thumbnail size. All lettering is a flat, front-facing overlay; no title is printed, painted, embossed, or perspective-warped onto a scene or object.

## New font sweep

### Cosette Marque — Cafespro Weekend

**Visible text:** `Cosette Marque` / `A High Contrast Editorial Serif`

**Prompt:** Preserve the specimen's exact editorial serif lettering. Set the large navy title on two lines across warm greige print paper, with the smaller rust-colored descriptor directly below. Place a faint, single-color printing-press line illustration at the far right as a secondary counterweight. Give the paper a subtle tooth and slightly uneven ink; keep the illustration quiet and leave clear air around every letter. No oversized cropped initial, orbit rings, split panel, arch, product photo, or extra copy.
