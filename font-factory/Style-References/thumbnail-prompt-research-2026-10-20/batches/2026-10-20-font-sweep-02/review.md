# Font Thumbnail Sweep 02 — Review Notes

This batch contains 10 fonts and 20 final renders: a separately composed 3:2 thumbnail and square thumbnail for every font. Each image was prompted with that font's actual white-background specimen as the lettering reference. Reference thumbnails informed the design mechanism only; they were not passed into the image model.

## What this batch explores

- Ten distinct visual mechanisms rather than one repeated “premium background” treatment: print, script-like brush marks, pixel art, movement photography, poster geometry, archival paper, projection graphic, botanical screenprint, sport photography, and paving texture.
- Both aspect ratios are intentionally composed as separate layouts. The square versions reframe the idea around their own title scale and subject placement.
- Each thumbnail carries only the font name and a readable Title Case descriptor. The typography stays in the foreground as a flat overlay; backgrounds do not carry printed lettering.
- Texture is tied to the material or subject: paper tooth, rough ink, pixel blocks, cloth movement, ledger fibers, track wear, or stone grain. No glitter or particle effects were used.
- Palette choices keep the title and descriptor distinct from their backgrounds. The images favor strong local contrast and open space around letterforms.

## Visual review

- Confirmed 20 final image files: 10 in `renders/3x2/` and 10 in `renders/1x1/`.
- Reviewed the generated images for title spelling, descriptor spelling, legibility, cropping, contrast, and whether the background crosses or embeds the title.
- Retained one final render per ratio per font; exploratory outputs are not in the batch render folders.
- The wide and square renders are shown as separate images in `index.html`, not combined into a collage.
