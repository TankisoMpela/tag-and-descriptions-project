# Thumbnail Batch 02 — Ten New Fonts

Each route uses only the matching white-background specimen as its lettering reference. Reference images below were studied for their compositional mechanism; none is supplied to the image model. Every thumbnail gets a separate 3:2 and square prompt, both with a readable Title Case descriptor.

## Shared image rules

- Exact font name in the actual font, preserving the specimen's natural title case and letterforms.
- Show only the font name and the stated descriptor. Set the descriptor in a clean supporting sans, Title Case, large enough to read at thumbnail size.
- Keep the name dominant, fully inside frame, sharp, flat, and front-facing. Treat it as an independent graphic overlay over any photo or illustration; never print it onto a scene, object, clothing, or surface.
- Give every glyph local contrast. Use restrained, subject-related texture and a font-specific visual idea. No glitter, particles, generic premium still-life setup, random decoration, extra copy, or fake glow.

## 1. Gligat — High-Contrast Serif

**Specimen:** `specimens/3x2/gligat.png` and `specimens/1x1/gligat.png`  
**Reference mechanism:** Alicent Hightower (#01): the title and its own flourishes carry the poster; broad print bands and ink texture support it. Keep the texture, leave the monogram and scattered detail behind.

**3:2 prompt:** Create a wide 3:2 printmaker's proof on deep petrol-blue stock, with two broad, irregular vermilion ink bands running vertically behind the center. Set the exact title “Gligat” in the supplied specimen's high-contrast serif, in title case, very large across the middle; keep the sweeping terminal of the G fully visible. Add “High-Contrast Serif” directly below in a clear small sans, still comfortably readable at thumbnail size. Use warm ivory for the name and quiet paper tooth with slight uneven ink at the edges. Let the specimen's own G supply the ornament. Keep all type flat, crisp, front-facing, and separate from the printed background. No other copy, monogram, stars, decorative swashes, fake depth, or cropped glyphs.

**1:1 prompt:** Compose a separate square screenprint: a warm-cream paper field, a single wide dark-teal ink strip crossing behind the upper half, and a narrow burnt-orange print edge along the left. Place the exact specimen-matched title “Gligat” as one large line just above center, preserving the curled G terminal, with “High-Contrast Serif” in a legible clean sans on a small, quiet cream line below. The title should occupy most of the width while retaining breathing room around the G. Add faint paper grain and one slightly misregistered edge to make the print feel handled. Keep the lettering flat and in front. No extra marks or copy.
