# Thumbnail Reference Review — Sequential Audit

Each of the 33 saved originals was opened individually, in index order, alongside its previous caption. The caption records visible content; the design analysis separates interpretation from observation; the prompt lesson extracts a principle without turning the image into a recipe.

## 01. Alicent Hightower

**Source image:** [01_T26543_00.webp](originals/01_T26543_00.webp)  
**Medium:** Designed type poster with distressed print texture  
**Visible copy:** Alicent Hightower · A Serif Typeface · Victoria Kingsley Design Studio · OpenType

### Full visual caption

A dark, symmetrical type poster layers alternating near-black vertical bands over a deep oxblood field, with dense red flecks and irregular distressed ink concentrated in broad columns. The two-line title ALICENT HIGHTOWER is set in very large warm-ivory high-contrast serif capitals across the centre. Long looping swashes are built into and around the letters themselves, so the title supplies the main ornament. A small circular engraved-style monogram sits at top centre; small sans-serif labels occupy the upper left and right, and the design-studio and OpenType labels anchor the lower corners. The lettering remains crisp against the granular ground. The image is a flat print-inspired composition, with no photographic subject, blur, or simulated depth.

### Style and use of elements

The vertical dark bands create a frame and rhythm behind the title; they do not compete as separate imagery. The palette stays tightly controlled, and the ornament is concentrated in the actual glyph construction. Corner copy gives the poster a finished specimen structure without challenging the title.

### Transferable prompting lesson

Use a print surface and one font-specific typographic gesture when they reinforce each other; keep extra ornament inside the title rather than scattering it around the frame.

## 02. Mirales
