The shared renderer ships 61 active supporting-image templates and reproducibly selects seven eligible layouts for a font. `plakat32`, `weightfamily32`, and `mangosboard32` require at least two real styles from one family; the two family boards are guaranteed in the seven when eligible, and `boots32` also requires multiple styles. The concepts (`announce32`, `ladder32`, `retro32`, `variable32`, `archive32`, `cast32`, `loop32`, `bondibeach32`, `sticker32`, `echo32`, `brutalpack32`, `dreamchalk32`, `lovecard32`, `queens32`, `lemonade32`, and `sunkiss32`) are withdrawn and unavailable to rendering; retired slots stay reserved to preserve stable filenames. The earlier experiments (`deboss32`, `anatomy32`, `riso32`, `shear32`, `mosaic32`, and `stencil32`) are also inactive.
