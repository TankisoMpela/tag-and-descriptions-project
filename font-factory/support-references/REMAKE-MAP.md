The active shared catalogue has 135 supporting-image templates; Agent 27 reproducibly selects 13 eligible layouts for each font. The family showcases `plakat32`, `weightfamily32`, `mangosboard32`, and `italicregular32` require at least two real styles from one family and are guaranteed in the 13 when eligible; `boots32` also requires multiple styles. The concepts (`announce32`, `ladder32`, `retro32`, `variable32`, `archive32`, `cast32`, `loop32`, `bondibeach32`, `sticker32`, `echo32`, `brutalpack32`, `dreamchalk32`, `lovecard32`, `queens32`, `lemonade32`, and `sunkiss32`) are withdrawn and unavailable to rendering; retired slots stay reserved to preserve stable filenames. The earlier experiments (`deboss32`, `anatomy32`, `riso32`, `shear32`, `mosaic32`, and `stencil32`) are also inactive. Blackletter-specific exclusions and the review are recorded in [blackletter-first-pass.md](class-audit-2026-10-14/blackletter-first-pass.md). Agent 27 uses [TEMPLATE-GUIDE.md](TEMPLATE-GUIDE.md) for caption, theme, contrast, and alt-text guidance.
