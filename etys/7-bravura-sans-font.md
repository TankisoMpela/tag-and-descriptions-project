## PRODUCT DESCRIPTION

Bravura Sans is a clean modern sans serif font designed for logos, branding, and professional design projects. Its sleek minimalist letterforms bring a refined contemporary look to any creative work while maintaining excellent readability across print and digital formats.

This modern sans serif typeface comes as a single Regular weight in OTF format and includes a full set of uppercase and lowercase letters, numerals, punctuation, and essential symbols. Beyond the standard character set, Bravura Sans features stylistic alternates and ligatures that give you flexibility to customize your typography. The font is PUA encoded so you can access every alternate glyph even in software that does not support OpenType features. Multilingual support covers a wide range of Latin-based languages.
